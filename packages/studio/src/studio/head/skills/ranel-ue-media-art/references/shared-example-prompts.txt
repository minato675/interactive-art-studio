Documentary photograph of a media art installation in a dark gallery, shot straight on at eye level from the central viewing point with a 24mm lens. A long, seamless LED wall, about twice the height of a person, fills most of the frame and works as a forced-perspective anamorphic 3D illusion: from this viewpoint the screen looks like the open front of a deep, dark stone chamber, its side walls, ceiling and floor clearly receding in perspective into darkness. Inside the chamber a colossal wave of dark water and liquid light surges forward from the back, crashes against the inner walls, and spills over the bottom edge of the screen as if pouring out toward the visitors' feet, with spray particles hanging in the air in front of the screen plane. Near droplets are large and softly out of focus, far details sharp and small. Ray-traced reflections and refraction, volumetric light. Black, white and deep cobalt. Visitors are small dark silhouettes, the polished black floor faintly reflects the glow. Cinematic, sculptural, museum-grade. Documentary photograph of a media art installation in a dark gallery, shot straight on at eye level from the central viewing point with a 24mm lens. A long, seamless LED wall, about twice the height of a person, fills most of the frame and works as a forced-perspective anamorphic 3D illusion: from this viewpoint the screen surface disappears into deep black space. Deep inside, a colossal cloud of pigment explodes in slow motion toward the viewer, like powdered ultramarine blue and vermilion red thrown into the air, billowing in huge three-dimensional plumes with fine grain detail. It starts as a small burst far inside and grows enormous as it approaches, its front edge pushing past the plane of the screen and seeming to spill into the gallery over the visitors. Everything else is pure black. Visitors are small dark silhouettes, the polished black floor reflects the color. Ray-traced light, strong depth of field. Cinematic, museum-grade. Documentary photograph of a media art installation in a dark gallery, shot from a low angle with a 24mm lens. A long, seamless LED wall, about twice the height of a person, fills the frame edge to edge and reads as a window into deep three-dimensional darkness. The room stays dark and plain; all the depth is inside the screen. Inside, one vast swarm of millions of tiny points of light moves like a murmuration, a single living volume that folds, stretches and spirals out of the far distance until it nearly reaches the front of the screen, then curls back into the depth. Near particles are large and softly out of focus, far particles tiny and sharp, so the swarm has unmistakable volume. Near each visitor the swarm bends away, leaving a hollow in their shape. Abstract, not literal. Pure white light with a faint thread of gold, mostly darkness. Avoid flat illustration, drawn lines, outlines, borders, 2D graphics, colorful fantasy, cartoon, video game look, neon, lens flares, text, logos.

Documentary photograph of a contemporary media art installation in a dark gallery, shot from a low angle with a 24mm lens. A long, seamless LED wall, about twice the height of a person, fills the frame from edge to edge and reads as a window into a deep three-dimensional night sea. A colossal whale, its body translucent like ink suspended in water and traced with fine lines of light, swims slowly along the full length of the wall, turning so that its enormous eye passes just behind the surface right in front of a visitor. Its fins and tail move with natural, weighty motion. Shafts of pale light fall through the water in volumetric beams, and tiny particles drift at many depths. Deep blue-black, white and a trace of silver, large areas of darkness. Long exposure: visitors are slightly blurred silhouettes, light spills onto them and reflects on a polished black concrete floor. Cinematic, sculptural, museum-grade. Avoid cartoon whale, aquarium photo, video game graphics, plastic CGI, neon, sparkles, lens flares, text, logos, trade show booth.

Documentary photograph of a media art installation in a dark gallery. A long, seamless LED wall, about twice the height of a person, fills the frame edge to edge, shot at eye level from the central viewing position with a 24mm lens. The room around the wall stays dark and plain; all the depth happens inside the screen. The screen shows a deep, dark stage space receding far back. From the back of this space, one gigantic three-dimensional flower slowly unfolds toward the front, its translucent petals of silk and thin glass overlapping in many layers, each layer closer, larger and sharper than the one behind. The nearest petals cross the inner edge of the screen so they seem to reach out toward the visitors. Petals near each visitor turn toward them. The far depth fades into crimson haze. Black, deep crimson and muted gold. Cinematic, sculptural, museum-grade. Avoid flat illustration, garden scenes, cartoon, video game graphics, plastic CGI, neon, sparkles, lens flares, text, logos, trade show booth.
